Company Annual Leave Policy

Employees receive 30 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should request annual leave through the company's HR system.

Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year, subject to company policy.

Employees should normally request leave at least two weeks in advance.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their agreed working hours.
